Why the bottom bar was floating too high on your iPhone 16 Pro:

In Safari on iOS 16/17/18, when the bottom URL tab bar is visible, height: 100dvh combined with justify-between and manual safe-area padding pushed the tab bar up above Safari's URL pill, creating a double-gap.

The Fix: We dock the tab bar with position: fixed; bottom: 0; and use box-sizing: content-box with padding-bottom: env(safe-area-inset-bottom, 0px). This sticks flush to Safari's address bar while in the browser, and flush above the iPhone gesture bar when installed as a PWA.
