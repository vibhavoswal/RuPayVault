How to switch views inside the app

Open RuPayVault anytime and tap the hamburger menu in the top-left corner. Under the Frontend Design View section, tap between Default, Cupertino iOS, Cupertino (Scratch), Obsidian OLED, or Swiss Precision to seamlessly switch views on the fly.
